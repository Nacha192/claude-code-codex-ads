# What this pack builds

**Motion creative: video ads, their scripts, their voice, their sound and their
assembly.** From the brief to a file somebody has actually watched.

Still images and carousels belong to the static packs in this same repository and
are not half-covered here. If the request is a single image ad, say so and use a
`meta-ads-static-*` edition instead of improvising from these references.

**No edition generates video by virtue of being installed.** Whether a video or
speech capability exists is a property of the machine it runs on, not of the
assistant reading this. Detect it, say what you found, and never describe a
script or a silent draft as a finished ad.

Three honest outcomes, all acceptable: a video capability exists and you generate
against a recorded approval; only a rendering toolchain exists and you do
deterministic motion, captions and assembly; neither exists and you deliver the
research, the script, the storyboard, the voice direction and the exact prompts,
saying plainly that nothing was generated.
