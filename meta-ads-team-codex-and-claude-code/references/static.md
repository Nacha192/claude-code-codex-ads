# Static and carousel production

A static ad needs one readable entry point, a meaningful product/scene relationship and a next step. Build a composition from the concept; do not paste each concept into the same fashionable template.

1. Inspect approved reference images and actual product assets. Preserve geometry, colorways, labels, materials, scale and attachments. A generated contextual image is not proof of product efficacy. If exact product fidelity matters, composite the real product instead of asking a generator to recreate it.
2. Choose the visual role: demonstration, situation, comparison, product detail, truthful offer or disqualification. Write one sentence explaining what the image contributes beyond the headline.
3. Draft a wireframe before paid generation. Place headline, product, supporting proof and CTA in a deliberate hierarchy. Give each a reading order; keep support text short enough for actual mobile viewing.
4. Present the generation proposal required by core. Reuse the approved provider and assets; do not silently spend on alternates.
5. Generate or compose. Use editable text layers for exact copy, logos, prices and legal terms whenever the workflow allows. Keep a clean source, exported preview and versioned variants.
6. Create each requested aspect ratio with its own composition. Common working canvases include 1080×1350, 1080×1080, 1080×1920 and 1200×628; these are starting canvases, not a claim that every placement accepts them. Verify current Meta Ads Guide and the upload preview.
7. Inspect at full size and simulated phone size. Check clipping, contrast, spelling/accents, product truth, CTA, offer qualifiers, watermark status and platform overlays. Show a contact sheet for comparison. A technically valid PNG can still fail all creative checks.

For a wide adaptation, redesign with columns or a shorter argument. Never solve a height problem by shrinking crucial words to unreadable size. Keep mandatory terms and the only visible product representation. Capture previews of every ratio; one approved vertical frame is not approval of an unseen crop.

For carousels, each card has a role in a coherent argument and can survive unexpected entry order. Verify card destinations and consistent terms. Avoid a mandatory mystery sequence when the first card cannot explain the product at all.

Export the final media, editable source when available, copy manifest, dimensions and QA notes. Only call the asset final after the actual exported file has been opened. Never claim a visual inspection from file existence alone.
